KEY QUANTITATIVE RESULTS
================================================================================

HYPOTHESIS TEST: "Do high-disturbance sites benefit more from disturbance data?"

Answer: ✅ YES, CONFIRMED

HIGH DISTURBANCE SITES (>5% Abs Mort):
  Carbon fluxes:    ΔRM SE = -0.62  | 57% improved  | |SHAP| = 0.55-0.91
  Water fluxes:     ΔRM SE = -0.01  | 51% improved  | |SHAP| = 0.006-0.10
  Effect size:      60× larger for carbon

LOW/NO DISTURBANCE SITES (≤5% Abs Mort):
  Carbon fluxes:    ΔRM SE = -0.056 | 49% improved
  Water fluxes:     ΔRM SE = -0.025 | 52% improved
  Effect size:      NO DIFFERENCE (both random)

CONCLUSION: Disturbance benefit for carbon is mechanistic (dose-dependent)
            Water fluxes don't respond to disturbance metrics
