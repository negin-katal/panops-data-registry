================================================================================

Task: Evaluate three different approaches to categorizing each disturbance 
      metric (Absolute Mortality, Relative Mortality, Relative Disturbance)

Sample: 164 sites (common between M06 and M08 models)

   ✓ Natural Breaks: 50-60-54 sites - Nearly optimal
   ✓ Equal-Width: 125-20-19 sites - POOR (extreme imbalance)

4. INTERPRETABILITY
   - Tertiles: "Low/Mid/High disturbance" = lowest/middle/highest 1/3
   - Natural Breaks: "Low/Mid/High" = below/within/above ±0.5SD of mean
   - Equal-Width: "Bin 1/2/3" = specific % ranges (hard to interpret)

================================================================================
RECOMMENDATIONS
================================================================================

RECOMMENDED: Use either TERTILES or NATURAL BREAKS

  TERTILES if:
    • You want equal statistical power across groups
    • You prefer simpler interpretation
    • You want to compare low vs mid vs high in balanced design
    
  NATURAL BREAKS if:
    • You want statistically grounded breaks
    • You prefer data-driven boundaries
    • You want to emphasize natural clustering

NOT RECOMMENDED: Do NOT use EQUAL-WIDTH
    • Extreme group size imbalance (76% vs 12% vs 12%)
    • Lowest category swallows majority of data
    • No meaningful comparison between groups
