🚨
CD-Ygb — VPD in Pa, not hPa
ICOS site CD-Ygb has VPD_mean up to 2109.0 in the monthly meteo. Values are in Pa — divide by 100 before use (→ 14–21 hPa, plausible for tropical DRC).
🚨
US-HB4 — Impossible precipitation
6 site-months with P_sum up to 39140.0 mm/month. World record is ~2,500 mm. Likely instrument error — exclude these months.
🚨
12 physically impossible EFP values
Negative uWUE / WUE in 6 sites (CA-PB1, US-EML, US-xMB, US-xML, US-xNW, US-xYE). Very small in magnitude — likely numerical artifacts at carbon balance limits.
🚨
GPPsat / uWUE / WUE — ~21.5% missing
Concentrated in 72 sites where Gavail=no (insufficient light-saturation data). EBF and DNF ecosystem classes are worst affected (~62% missing).
⚠️
CN-Sb1 & CN-Sb2 — extreme WUE / uWUE outliers
WUE up to 34× and uWUE up to 17× the global median (z-scores 6–14). Likely rice paddy / managed cropland. Consider excluding or flagging in sensitivity analysis.
⚠️
KOF KR-SmM & TERN AU-How — no NT GPP partitioning
Only GPP_DT_VUT_REF available. EFP computation falls back to DT partitioning for these sites.
⚠️
AMF — hourly resolution, not half-hourly
AmeriFlux delivers _HR_ files. Any n_obs ≥ 1488 threshold in EFP scripts should be halved (~744) for AMF sites.
✓
All other unit checks passed
TA within −60 to +55 °C · SW_IN within 0–1000 W m⁻² · VPD within expected range in all 9 network spot-checks (excluding CD-Ygb) · No negative ET in monthly aggregates.
